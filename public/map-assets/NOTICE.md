# Offline map geography

`sea-land-v1.json` is a clipped, generalized and Mercator-projected extraction of Natural Earth's 1:10 million land geometry. It covers Southeast Asia (90–125°E, 11°S–25°N). It is an overview coastline, not cadastral or street-level data. Zooming does not improve source accuracy.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_land.geojson

Original source SHA-256: `1ac90796408bc6ad6911d69448485d3c4dbf2190370080368a09976e1c9f7416`.

Natural Earth places all versions of its vector and raster map data in the public domain, including modification, redistribution and commercial use: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . No Google or OpenStreetMap tiles, imagery or street data are included. Project coordinates are separately attributed facts and are not inferred from this land geometry.
